### Word counts and pace

Roughly 6,400 spoken words across 60 minutes ≈ **107 words per minute** —
deliberately slow. The pauses are the design. A presenter running at 140 wpm
will finish in 46 minutes and the reflection moments will not have happened.

**If you are running behind at minute 33** (the drift point): cut what-if
scenarios 4 and 6. Recovers 2 min 20 sec.

**Never cut:** Slide 3 (trust line), Slide 7 (one chapter), Slide 15 (cannot),
the anti-sunk-cost paragraph on Slide 17, the eight-second silence on Slide 18.
Overrun the close instead.

# §28 · Visual Brief for Every Slide

## Design direction

**Cinematic · Premium · Personal · Scientific · Emotional** — in that priority
order. The deck should look closer to a documentary title sequence than to a
pharma presentation.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Ground** | Deep indigo, near-black (`#141024`–`#1C1533`). Warm cream for light slides (`#F7F3EC`) |
| **Accent** | A single warm gold (`#C9A227`) for emphasis. Teal (`#2E7D74`) for process chains only |
| **Type** | One serif for headlines (Fraunces / Playfair), one humanist sans for body (Inter / Source Sans). Devanagari: Mukta or Noto Sans Devanagari — check the Devanagari cut renders at large sizes before finalising |
| **Rule** | **One idea per slide.** If a slide needs two sentences to explain, it is two slides |
| **Images** | Human, domestic, unposed, warm. Real Indian homes and kitchens, not stock "wellness" |
| **Motion** | Cross-dissolve only, 400ms. No wipes, no spins, no build-ins except where specified |

Phenotype grids · disease-list walls · stock laboratory photography · glowing

## Three visual decisions worth defending

**Slide 12 has no science imagery at all.** Six photographs of ordinary
domestic moments. The emotional peak of a genetics session should not contain a
single image of genetics — the whole point of that section is that this is
about a Tuesday evening, not a laboratory.

**Slide 15 looks like a statement, not a disclaimer.** Same type size, same
treatment, same confidence as every value slide. Formatting the limitations as
fine print would undo exactly what the slide exists to do.

**Slide 18's seven cards are visually identical.** No highlight, no ordering, no
"most common" label. If "पैसा" is styled differently from "डर", the audience
reads a judgement and the private reflection turns into a performance.
